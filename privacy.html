Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

ArsenalGamer Uploader ("the App") is a private tool operated by the owner of the online store Arsenal Gamer. Its only purpose is to upload videos created by our team to our own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube and Instagram). The App is not offered to the public and does not have end users other than the account owner.

Information we access

When the account owner connects one of their own social media accounts, the App receives, through the platform's official login (OAuth):

  • Basic profile information of the connected account (for example display name and account ID).
  • An access token that allows the App to upload and publish videos to that account.
  • The publishing status of the videos it uploads.

The App does not access followers' data, direct messages, contacts or any information about other users.

How we use it

  • Only to upload, publish and check the status of videos on the account owner's own accounts.
  • We never sell, rent or share this information with anyone for marketing purposes.

Storage and third-party services

Access tokens are stored securely and are handled by our integration provider (Composio), which connects the App to each platform's official API. Videos are sent directly to the platform where they are published. We keep this information only while the account remains connected; disconnecting the account in the App or revoking access in the platform's settings deletes it.

Your rights

Since the only connected accounts belong to the App owner, there is no third-party personal data to request or delete. If you have any question about this policy, contact us through arsenalgamer.store/pages/contact.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
